About

Tilikiwei Doumobowei Precious is a student of History and International Studies at Delta State University, Abraka, Delta State, Nigeria. His interests sit at the intersection of history, international relations, diplomacy and public policy.

He is from Warri North Local Government Area of Delta State, was born in Ajakurama Community, Ovia South-West Local Government Area of Edo State, and grew up in Sapele, Delta State. These experiences have contributed to his interest in history, society, governance and international affairs.

He aspires to contribute to scholarship, diplomacy and evidence-informed public policy, with particular attention to the ways historical knowledge and international engagement can help societies understand conflict, cooperation and change.
