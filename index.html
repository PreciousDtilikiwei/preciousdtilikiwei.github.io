A little about me

History as a guide to the future.

I am Tilikiwei Doumobowei Precious, a graduate of History and International Studies at Delta State University, Abraka, Nigeria. My interests bring together historical research, international relations, diplomacy, governance and public policy.

My academic journey has strengthened my interest in how communities preserve memory, how institutions and states respond to conflict, and how historical understanding can inform contemporary decisions. I hope to keep developing these interests through research, writing, practical learning and public engagement.

I am from Warri North Local Government Area of Delta State, was born in Ajakurama community in Ovia South-West Local Government Area of Edo State, and grew up in Sapele, Delta State. These connections have helped shape my interest in the histories, cultures and experiences of the Niger Delta and Nigeria.

Research & intellectual interests

Questions worth preserving and exploring.

My research interests centre on African history, indigenous knowledge, cultural preservation, conflict and the relationship between historical experience and public life.

Academic research project

The Historical and Cultural Significance of Egbesu Divinity Worship among the Egbema Ijaw People of Delta and Edo States

This research examines the place of Egbesu worship in Egbema Ijaw historical experience and cultural life, with attention to cultural identity, continuity and preservation. The project reflects my interest in documenting community histories and taking indigenous perspectives seriously.
